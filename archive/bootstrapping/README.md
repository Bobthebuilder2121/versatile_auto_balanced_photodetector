# Historical Bootstrapping Work

The April 2026 presentation, LTspice cases, experiments, plots, and early requirement notes are preserved here for provenance. Flying rails and bootstrap autobalancing are not part of the current architecture. See [active requirements](../../requirements/requirements.md) and [architecture decision](../../notes/architecture.md).

Generated LTspice `.raw`, `.log`, `.net`, and exported waveform `.txt` files remain on disk but are ignored by Git. Third-party amplifier `.lib` models are also local-only; obtain them from their vendors to rerun archived schematics. Schematics, plots, and notes are versioned.
